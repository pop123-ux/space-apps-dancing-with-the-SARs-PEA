### What the page does now

- **Opens on Ocnele Mari** (D-002) with Romania blurred below MapLibre zoom 10, and the town's administrative boundary drawn as a black line (OpenStreetMap relation 9845422).
- **Satellite menu:** NISAR (catalog coverage only, "Displacement fit pending"), Sentinel-1 (measured), Compare (disabled with its reason until NISAR is measured).
- **Views:** Change and Rate draw Sentinel-1 as dots, one every 80 m, coloured red (away from the satellite) to blue (toward); a click shows the value.
