# AI disclosure

**Owner:** all roles append to the log; 01 finalises section 1

This file says how we used AI tools, which Space Apps 2026 calls "Super Intelligence" (SI), to build **Dancing on Salt** for NASA Space Apps 2026 (Cluj-Napoca, 14 to 15 Nov 2026), and what people did and checked. Section 1 is the text for the project page. The log in section 5 is the record it is built from.

**The rules we follow.** The 2026 [Project Submission Guide](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/project-submission-guide/) (read 15 Nov 2026) asks, in the field "Use of Super Intelligence (SI)", whether and how SI tools were used. SI tools such as machine learning, chatbots, image generation and large language models are allowed as tools, but not to come up with the idea of how to solve the challenge. Where SI was used must be clearly indicated: images and videos with a visible watermark; audio, code and data acknowledged in descriptive text and metadata. Generated content may not use NASA branding. The field's answer is for data collection only; the judges see the SI indicators inside the project.

**Everyone appends to the log** (section 5) when an AI tool produces code, text, figures or analysis that ends up in the project, and says what a person checked. Never rewrite someone else's line.

The maps and charts are drawn from data, not generated. We made no video or audio. A team member asked for every change and reviewed the result, and every substantive use is logged with what a person checked in `AI-DISCLOSURE.md` in our repository.

## 3. Where SI was not used, and how its use is marked

- **The idea.** The site, the question and the plan came from the team and our advisor's plan of 12 Nov 2026 (`CITATIONS.md`, "Prior work"), not from SI.
- **Measurements.** No measured number on the page, on the slides or in the submission is produced or adjusted by SI. They all come from `products/ocnele_mari/`, written by our scripts (`CLAUDE.md`, "Honest numbers"). The only SI-produced numbers are the Chronos-2 predictions, and they are labelled as predictions.
- **Images, video and audio.** The map overlays and charts are computed from data. We made no video or audio. The logo's origin is still to be confirmed (section 1).
- **NASA branding.** No SI-generated content uses NASA logos, flags or mission identifiers.
- **Decisions.** Team decisions (one site, the map opening on the town, the 7-slide submission, two radars with one switch) were made by people and are logged in `docs/build-spec.md`. SI sessions record their small working choices in `DECISIONS.md`, where anyone can see and overturn them.
- **Accounts.** People log in themselves. Where a session downloaded data, it used a token saved in `.env` or a stored credential, without displaying it (log rows of 15 Nov).
- **Where the marks are.** In descriptive text: this file and the README's "How we used AI". In metadata: the commit lines naming Claude as co-author, and the model name stored in each forecast file.

## 4. How people checked SI output

- **Ownership:** each file has one owner (`CLAUDE.md`, "Who owns what"), who asks for, reviews and merges the changes to it.
- **Tests:** `pytest -q` runs before every push and in GitHub Actions on every push (`.github/workflows/tests.yml`).
- **Contract:** `checkpoint.py` checks that the products match the file contracts.
- **Independent validation:** the validator (05) owns `validate.py`, which no one else edits: the sign of the motion, held-out GNSS stations, the tie, NISAR against Sentinel-1. The current check results were run by the 02/03 session and are marked as a draft until 05 reviews them.
- **Synthetic tests:** 03 ran a synthetic stack with a known answer on the real NISAR dates (`notebooks/03/t5_t6_synthetic.py`). The full known-answer demo site, with a planted lying station, is not built.
- **Red team and audits:** 05 red-teamed the pin (`notebooks/05/pin_redteam.py`), 04 mock-judged the page against the five criteria, and 06 audited the data and the backend on 15 Nov: the NISAR and Sentinel-1 inputs are real, and the Sentinel-1 products rebuild exactly (`logs/06-app.md`).
